## My Favorite Movie: Spider-Man: Brand New Day

**Genre:** Action, Adventure, Superhero
**Director:** Destin Daniel Cretton
**Release date:** July 31, 2026
**Main actor:** Tom Holland as Peter Parker / Spider-Man

### What it's about

The story takes place four years after *Spider-Man: No Way Home*. Everyone forgot who Peter Parker is, so he is now fighting crime in New York **alone**. Then a strange new pattern of crimes shows up, and Peter has to face one of the most powerful threats he has ever seen.

### Main cast

1. Tom Holland as Peter Parker / Spider-Man
2. Zendaya as MJ
3. Jacob Batalon as Ned
4. Jon Bernthal as Frank Castle / The Punisher
5. Sadie Sink
6. Mark Ruffalo as Bruce Banner / The Hulk

### Why I like it

- Spider-Man is a hero who feels like a normal person
- The action scenes look amazing
- It has a good mix of humor and emotion
- Seeing Spider-Man go up against the Hulk and the Punisher
